**Discrepancy explicitly flagged, per the task's instruction not to silently
reconcile:** `tab:s-weakreal` (the Wilson-CI table the main text abstract and
Section 3.2 actually cite) reports **mean recovery = 0.604** (macro-average of
the six per-organism values: .656/.560/.633/.613/.520/.641 = 0.604) and
**pooled recovery = 0.601** (368/612, i.e. pooling all six organisms' W sets
before dividing, rather than averaging six ratios). `tab:s-weakreal-matched`
(the size-matched-control table this panel's METEOR/top-K points are drawn
from) lists a very slightly different per-organism METEOR column
(.656/.560/.633/.613/.**525**/.**647**, note S. aureus and B. subtilis differ
in the fourth-decimal from `tab:s-weakreal`'s .520/.641), whose macro-average
is **0.606**. So there are three numbers in play for "METEOR's mean recall of
W": 0.601 (pooled), 0.604 (macro-avg, tab:s-weakreal), 0.606 (macro-avg,
tab:s-weakreal-matched) -- the pooled-vs-macro-average difference (0.601 vs
0.604) is an expected and unremarkable effect of averaging method, but the
0.604-vs-0.606 difference reflects the two supplementary tables giving
slightly different S. aureus/B. subtilis values for what should be the
identical METEOR-recovers-W quantity. This panel plots the
`tab:s-weakreal-matched` (0.606) numbers because that table has a matching raw
JSON file (`weakreal_matched.json`) to verify against; the 0.604 vs 0.606
discrepancy between the two tables is stated here rather than silently
resolved in either direction.
